# Grasp

@GRASP.md

GRASP.md is the HackPSU brief and the source of truth. Update it when a decision changes.

## Build and run

- Open `Grasp.xcodeproj`. Schemes: **GraspMac** (the canvas, run on My Mac) and **GraspPhone** (the eye).
- Mac app from the command line: `xcodebuild -project Grasp.xcodeproj -scheme GraspMac -destination 'platform=macOS' build`
- iPhone app compile check: `xcodebuild -project Grasp.xcodeproj -scheme GraspPhone -destination 'generic/platform=iOS Simulator' build`
- Shared logic tests: `cd GraspKit && swift test`
- Live Gemini test (reads `Fixtures/contract-photo.jpg`): `GEMINI_API_KEY=... swift test --filter LiveGemini` from `GraspKit/`. Skipped without the key.
- Test the phone link without an iPhone: run GraspPhone in the simulator; it sends numbered test frames.
- Printable demo props live in `props/` (contract, window sticker, credit union quote, phone plan). In the simulator, the phone's shutter sends the first three in turn. `props/lecture-cooling.mp4` is a 62 s narrated lecture for trying videos (`props/lecture/make-lecture.sh` remakes it; needs ffmpeg).
- The product is one engine, not modes: any page → document card (facts, catches, model) with provenance on hover; Ask (fist or ⌘K) answers from the pages with Swift math. Keep new features inside that engine.
- Check the Mac UI without screen recording: launch with `open -n --env GRASP_SNAPSHOT=/tmp/shot.png [--env GRASP_SNAPSHOT_DELAY=6] [--env GRASP_SNAPSHOT_SETUP=…] <Grasp.app>` and read the PNG; the app quits once it's saved. Setup steps: `demo`, `term48`, `quote`, `compare`, `lines`, `advice`, `summary`, `read:/path/page.jpg`, `ask:question`, `hover`, `hovertotal`, `hoveranswer`, `hovernote2` (passage behind note 2), `lab` (write up the rehearsal session), `hoverstep4` (frame and reading for notebook step 4), `temps`, `pour:A`, `lid:B`, `ice:C` (act on the simulated bench), `wait30`, `comparecups`, `guide`, `next`, `finish` (a guided protocol run), `method`, `related`, `hoverwork1`, `dispute`, `hovercited3` (the third thing the newest research or letter card cites), `hovercatch2` (the second catch on the newest page), `dive` (deep dive on what's hovered, else the card in focus), `putaway` (thumbs up on the card in focus), `note:text` (a line on the note pad beside the page in focus), `checknotes`, `editnotes`, `video:/path.mp4`, `youtube:link`, `hovermoment2` (the second moment of the newest video), `plan` (what to do about the page in focus), `hoverplan2` (its second step), `labstart`, `labstop` (writes what was heard to `<snapshot>-transcript.txt`). ⌘⇧S saves a snapshot to the Desktop. Copy photos out of `~/Documents` (to /tmp) before `read:`, or the launched app waits on a folder-access prompt.
- Mouse and keyboard stand-ins for every gesture are in the Canvas menu (⌘[ ⌘] term, ⌘L bars/lines, ⌘M compare, ⌘U demo quote, ⌘T temperatures, ⌫ delete card, ⌘↓ put away = thumbs up, ⌘⇧D deep dive = two hands zooming in). Make a fist or hold Space to talk.
- Earlier cards fold into chips down the left, one group per page (its answers, notes, dives beside it); the loans, stickers and what-to-say share a "Car deal" group. Clicking a chip brings its whole group back.
- ⌘O reads a photo or a video; ⌘⇧Y studies a YouTube link. A video becomes a page (moments, facts at their times, notes stamped with the playing time).
- Money menu: ⌘J what to say (spoken by ElevenLabs), ⌘⇧L listen to the salesperson (asks for consent), ⌘E decision summary PDF, ⌘⇧A what to do about this page. Snapshot runs stay silent.
- Every page with money on it gets a "What to do" card on its own (`PagePlanner`, `ActionPlanner`): how it compares with the market (web, sourced), steps tied to its catches, and a bottom line Swift checks against the steps' amounts.
- Everything spoken goes through `SpokenText.words` first: the fast ElevenLabs model misreads digits, so money, percents, decimals, times and years go in as words.
- During a guided run the bench camera (phone, else Mac) is checked every 5 s (`BenchWatcher`): a step the probes can't check finishes when two looks in a row see it done; something done differently is said and noted.
- Research menu: ⌘⇧N write notes on the page (a pad beside it; "Check" links each line to the page or video), ⌘⇧R start/stop lab notes, Rehearse a Lab Session, ⌘⇧E save the lab notebook to the Desktop, ⌘⇧G start the protocol, ⌘⏎ next step, ⌘⇧⏎ previous step, ⌘⇧M map the method, ⌘⇧F find related work, ⌘⇧B compare my cups with the paper. Reading a paper starts its live model; reading a protocol shows its steps.
- Bench menu: pour, lid or ice any simulated cup (markers land on the live chart). Money menu adds ⌘⇧W write the dispute and ⌘⇧C copy the letter.
- The bench hardware (Pico W wiring, firmware, Mac serial reader) is in the hardware guide: https://claude.ai/artifact/HmbjbSeNhPi5QqThP99vkF. The Pico firmware is `firmware/pico/main.py` (save it to the Pico as `main.py` from Thonny). The Mac reads it with `GraspMac/Hardware/SerialPort.swift` and `SerialProbeSource.swift`: `ProbeHub` runs simulated probes until the board answers `PING` (or sends its `T` lines), then switches to it. Quit Thonny before running Grasp; only one program can hold the Pico's USB port.
- Live test batteries (need `GEMINI_API_KEY`): `TestDocumentTests` (the planted mistakes in `props/photos`, and no false alarms), `FollowUpTests` (four-question chains per document), `NotACarTests`, `LiveNoteLinkerTests`, `LiveLabNotebookTests`, `LiveModelReaderTests`, `LiveProtocolReaderTests`, `LiveMethodReaderTests`, `LiveRelatedWorkTests` (also needs the network, for OpenAlex), `LiveDisputeWriterTests`, `LiveDeepDiveTests` (Google Search grounding), `LiveVideoReaderTests` (watches `props/lecture-cooling.mp4`), `LiveYouTubeTests` (a YouTube link), `LiveVoiceTests`, `LiveActionPlannerTests`, `LiveBenchWatcherTests` (a drawn bench in `Fixtures/bench-ice-in-c.jpg`), `LiveSpokenNumbersTests` (ElevenLabs says numbers, Gemini writes down what it heard; needs both keys). `LiveTigerTests` runs with `TIGER_SERVICE=user:password@host:port/db` in the environment. Answer key: `props/TEST-DOCUMENTS.md`.
- Live ElevenLabs test: `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=... swift test --filter LiveElevenLabs` (costs ~30 characters).
- Keys: copy `Config/Secrets.example.xcconfig` to `Config/Secrets.xcconfig` (git-ignored) and fill it in. Read them in code with `APIKeys.gemini` / `APIKeys.elevenLabs` / `APIKeys.tigerService`. `TIGER_SERVICE` is the Tiger Cloud connection string without `postgres://` (xcconfig reads `//` as a comment); without it, saving is off.
- Tiger Data: service `db-48468` (id `ces0b6apcy`), reached with the `tiger` CLI. Look at what Grasp saved with `tiger db query ces0b6apcy --read-only --command "SELECT …"` (tables `grasp_pages`, `grasp_answers`, `grasp_notes`, `grasp_deep_dives`, `grasp_lab_sessions`, hypertable `grasp_readings`; for judges: `SELECT time_bucket('1 minute', time) AS minute, cup, round(avg(celsius)::numeric, 1) FROM grasp_readings GROUP BY minute, cup ORDER BY minute DESC`). Live test: `TIGER_SERVICE="$(tiger db uri ces0b6apcy --with-password)" swift test --filter LiveTigerTests`. Tiger signs with its own certificate authority, so `sslmode=require` encrypts without checking the certificate, as psql does.

## Conventions

- Swift 5 language mode, SwiftUI, `@Observable`, no third-party dependencies.
- Numbers are computed in Swift (`GraspKit/Math`), never by the AI.
- Pure logic both apps can use (math, gestures, link messages) lives in GraspKit with tests. Camera, Vision and UI code live in the app targets.
- The app folders are folder-synced groups: a new file in `GraspMac/` or `GraspPhone/` joins its target automatically, with no project edits.
- The user makes every git commit. Never commit; list the changed files and suggest a message instead.
