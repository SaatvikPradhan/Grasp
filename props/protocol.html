Protocol: how much does a lid slow cooling?Intro Physics Lab · Station 4 · 15 minutes

You will measure how fast hot water cools in three paper cups: one left open, one with a lid, and one with ice added. The probes read once a second.

Materials

  • Three 350 mL paper cups on the mat at A, B and C, one plastic lid
  • Kettle of hot water (about 90 °C), two ice cubes
  • Three temperature probes, labelled A, B and C

Procedure

  1. Place the three cups on the mat at A, B and C and clip a probe into each, 2 cm above the bottom.
  2. Pour 350 mL of hot water into cup A. Leave it open.
  3. Pour 350 mL of hot water into cup B and put the lid on straight away.
  4. Pour 350 mL of hot water into cup C.
  5. Wait until cup C has cooled below 85 °C.
  6. Drop two ice cubes into cup C. Do not stir.
  7. Leave all three cups untouched for 2 minutes.
  8. Read out the final temperature of each cup.
Safety. Hot water scalds: hold cups by the rim and pour slowly. Keep water away from the laptop. Wipe up spills straight away.
