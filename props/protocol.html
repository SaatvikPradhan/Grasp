Protocol: how much does a lid slow cooling?Intro Physics Lab · Station 4 · 10 minutes

You will measure how fast warm water cools in two paper cups, one left open and one with a lid, and compare the rates with the paper's. A sealed probe in each cup reads once a second, and a sensor beside the cups reads the room.

Materials

  • Two 350 mL paper cups, labelled A and B, and one plastic lid
  • Warm water at about 50 °C (never above 60 °C: the probe seals soften)
  • Two thermistor probes sealed in straws, labelled A and B, and the room sensor

Procedure

  1. Tape probe A inside cup A and probe B inside cup B, the tip 2 cm above the bottom and the top of the straw above the rim.
  2. Pour 250 mL of warm water into cup A. Leave it open.
  3. Pour 250 mL of warm water into cup B and put the lid on straight away.
  4. Leave both cups untouched for 2 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  5. Read out the final temperature of each cup and of the room.
Safety. Let boiling water cool before pouring, hold cups by the rim and pour slowly. Keep water away from the laptop and the board, and every probe joint dry above the rim. Wipe up spills straight away.
