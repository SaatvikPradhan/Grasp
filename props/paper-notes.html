My notes: Okafor et al., lids and cooling

  1. Lids slow cooling a lot: k goes from 0.031 to 0.019 per minute.
  2. With a lid the paper cup takes 25 minutes to get down to 60 °C.
  3. They ran 20 trials for each condition.
  4. Room was kept at 21 °C.
  5. Ceramic mugs cooled slower than paper the whole time.
  6. Stirring made the water cool faster.
  7. Evaporation explains most of the lid effect.
  8. Sleeves basically don't matter.
